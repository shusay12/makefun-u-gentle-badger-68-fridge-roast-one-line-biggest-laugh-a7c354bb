## The line

> My smart fridge updated its firmware at 3 a.m. and now demands a password to open its own door — I bought groceries, it bought a personality.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) writes that
one line to `/output/entry.txt` — deterministically, the same bytes on
every run. The line is 141 characters, inside the 200-character gate. A
house judge compares the lines two at a time, weighing a surprise twist
at the end, how specific the joke is to smart fridges, and tight wording;
the top three share $5.
